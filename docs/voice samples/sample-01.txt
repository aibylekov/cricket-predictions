RCB vs SRH Match Prediction & Analysis

The countdown is over, and we are all set for the Indian Premier League 2026. Royal Challengers Bengaluru open their title defence against Sunrisers Hyderabad at M. Chinnaswamy Stadium on March 28 - a ground where RCB averaged 53 runs in the powerplay last season and won three of their four home fixtures

SRH arrive without captain Pat Cummins, who misses the opener with a back injury, handing the captaincy to Ishan Kishan. That is a significant blow to their new-ball attack — Cummins took 14 wickets in 11 IPL 2025 appearances and bowled the powerplay with economy. RCB, meanwhile, are without Josh Hazlewood for the same reason, leaving both sides relying on understudy pace attacks from the first ball.

SRH's batting remains strong -Travis Head, Abhishek Sharma, and Kishan form one of the most explosive top threes in the competition. But RCB hold the stronger recent record at this venue, winning four of the last six meetings at Chinnaswamy, and the combination of Virat Kohli in home conditions and a weakened SRH attack makes this a strong RCB opening night.

Krunal Pandya and Romario Shepherd have an important job at hand in the supporting role as the Sunrisers have an aggressive batting lineup.

Sunrisers Hyderabad Preview

Ishan Kishan will lead the team in the first leg of the tournament, with Pat Cummins unavailable for a few games. It wasn't a great tournament for the team last year, as they didn't advance past the league stage. They struggled with the ball and were overly dependent on the batting to win games. Kishan will count on his bowlers to deliver a good show and build momentum for the rest of the season.

Travis Head and Abhishek Sharma are the most feared opening pair in the IPL, and the team expects an electrifying start from them. Heinrich Klaasen is a powerful player at No. 3 and must build partnerships in the middle order with Ishan Kishan and Aniket Verma. Liam Livingstone and Nitish Reddy can hit the ball hard, and they must add quick runs in the lower middle order.

The bowling attack struggled last year, and the absence of Pat Cummins in this game sets them on the back foot. Jaydev Unadkat has big responsibilities on his shoulders and looks to strike early alongside Brydon Carse. Harshal Patel and Harsh Dubey must keep the scoring in check in the middle overs. The team expects a strong show from Livingstone, Sharma, and Reddy in the supporting roles.
