TEAMS PREVIEW

Royal Challengers Bengaluru Preview

Royal Challengers Bengaluru kicked off their campaign on a high, beating Sunrisers Hyderabad at home in convincing fashion as they chased down 202 in just 15.4 overs with four wickets left. It was a commanding effort that set the tone early in the season.

Bengaluru got contributions from all areas, with Virat Kohli scoring 69 off 38 and Devdutt Padikkal making 61 off 26, as the pair dominated up front and made the chase look comfortable. Rajat Patidar added the finishing touch with a quick 31 off only 12 balls. Their top order looks well settled with Kohli, Philip Salt, Patidar, and Padikkal, while Jitesh Sharma, Tim David, and Romario Shepherd add strength further down.

Kohli will again be one to watch in this contest, given his record against CSK, against whom he has scored 1,146 runs at an average of 38.2, along with 10 fifties over the years.

Tim David adds serious hitting ability in the middle overs and has a strike rate of 172 in T20s since 2025. Romario Shepherd has also been very effective, striking close to 175 in the same period. Both can shift the game quickly in the final overs.

Kiwi pacer Jacob Duffy made an instant impression in the last match, picking up 3 for 22, and could play a big role this season, especially against CSK’s top order that has struggled at times. Bhuvneshwar Kumar also kept things tight in the previous game, which adds value on batting-friendly tracks. RCB will also look to spinners Suyash Sharma and Krunal Pandya in the middle overs, and their contest against Shivam Dube could be worth keeping an eye on, as he prefers to attack spin and does not hold back.

Chennai Super Kings Preview

Chennai Super Kings have not started well this season, losing by eight wickets to Rajasthan Royals in Guwahati and then going down again against Punjab Kings at home.

Their batting looked better in the last game as CSK posted 209, but the bowlers failed to defend it as Punjab chased it down without much trouble. They will need a stronger overall display, esepcially in the bowling, as they get ready to face a confident RCB side at their home ground.

Sanju Samson and Ruturaj Gaikwad are expected to provide a good start at the top. Samson was in excellent form during the recent T20 World Cup, scoring 321 runs in five innings at a strike rate close to 200. He has not delivered in the first two games, but this surface could suit his attacking style. Gaikwad, who leads the side, will look to hold one end and then shift gears once he gets set.

Ayush Mhatre and Sarfaraz Khan, who could be used as the Impact Sub, add depth to the middle order. Both made useful contributions in the previous game, with Mhatre scoring 73 off 43 and Sarfaraz adding a quick 32 off 12 to lift the scoring rate.

Shivam Dube, who made an unbeaten 45 off 27 in the last match, will look to take on the spinners during the middle overs. He plays that role in the middle order and has a strike rate of 152 at the M Chinnaswamy Stadium, which makes him a big threat against RCB’s spin attack.

The pace trio of Anshul Kamboj, Matt Henry, and Khaleel Ahmed have the ability but need to execute better on this kind of pitch. Henry looks the most reliable and could have a big say against Virat Kohli, Philip Salt, and Rajat Patidar. The Patidar and Henry contest is worth watching, as the batter averages only 23.52 against pace in the IPL. Noor Ahmad could also make an impact with his four overs of spin.
