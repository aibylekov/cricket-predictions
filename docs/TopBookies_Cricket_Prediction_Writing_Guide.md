# TopBookies — Cricket Prediction Writing Guide

> **Purpose of this file:** This is the canonical writing standard for every cricket prediction article published on TheTopBookies. Follow this structure, section order, word counts, and rules exactly when writing or reviewing any cricket prediction. When a request conflicts with this guide, follow this guide unless the user explicitly overrides it.

---

## 1. Prediction Structure (article opening)

### 1.1 Title
Create a compelling title that summarizes the match in a single sentence.

**Examples:**
- Can Bangladesh Stop the Indian Test Juggernaut?
- Cape Town Aim for a Strong Comeback Against Jozi Stars in the Mzansi Super League!
- India and New Zealand Battle It Out in the Series Decider
- Can New Zealand Defy the Odds and Clinch the Series?

**Title guidelines:**
- Prefer question-based headlines when the match has high stakes.
- Highlight any of: series deciders, must-win games, rivalries, qualification scenarios, tournament implications.

### 1.2 Three Key Points
List **three strong reasons** why your predicted winner is likely to win.

Each reason should draw on a real angle such as: current form, venue advantage, or key player statistics.

**Possible data sources:** tournament statistics, head-to-head records, venue records, individual player performances.

**Example:**
1. Team A has won four of their last five matches.
2. Teams batting first have won 60% of matches at this venue.
3. Player X has scored 320 runs in the tournament at an average of 53.33.

### 1.3 Our Prediction Is…
Clearly state the pick:

> **Our Prediction: Team A to Win**

## 2. Match Preview
This section sets the tone for the prediction.

**Must include:**
- Current team standings
- Importance of the game
- Brief overview of both teams

### 2.1 About This Match (approx. 150 words)
Provide detailed context covering:

- **Tournament situation:** current positions, recent performances, momentum.
- **Match importance:** qualification implications, knockout race, series outcome, dead rubber status.
- **Interesting storylines:** rivalries, key players returning, winning streaks, historic records.

---

## 3. Host Team Preview (approx. 200 words)

Write the section in this order:

1. **Last Match Performance** — start with how the team performed in its most recent game.
2. **Tournament Performance** — overall record, recent form, strengths and weaknesses.
3. **Team News** — injuries, suspensions, expected lineup changes, tactical adjustments.
4. **Key Players to Watch** — include at least one betting player prop.
   - Example: Virat Kohli Over 34.5 Runs @ 1.85 (1xBet)
   - Example: Jasprit Bumrah Over 1.5 Wickets @ 2.00 (4rabet)
5. **Venue Statistics** — record at venue, average score, batting-first performance, chasing performance.

### 3.1 Expected Playing XI
List the officially announced or expected starting eleven (11 players).

**Example:**
1. Player A
2. Player B
3. Player C
4. Player D
5. Player E
6. Player F
7. Player G
8. Player H
9. Player I
10. Player J
11. Player K

---

## 4. Guest Team Preview (approx. 200 words)

Follow **exactly the same structure** as the Host Team Preview.

**Required sections:**
- Last Match Performance
- Tournament Form
- Team News
- Key Players to Watch
- Betting Tip
- Venue Statistics
- Expected Playing XI

---

## 5. Pitch & Weather Report

### 5.1 Weather Conditions (approx. 50 words)
Focus **only** on weather.

- **Include:** temperature, rain probability, humidity, wind conditions, dew factor.
- **Mention:** hot / cold conditions, dry or humid weather, impact on bowlers or batters.
- ❌ Do **not** discuss pitch characteristics here.

### 5.2 Venue & Pitch Analysis (approx. 50 words)
Answer the following:

- **Is the venue batting-friendly?**
- **Average First Innings Score** — include tournament average, format average, and similar tournaments if required.
- **Pitch type** — pace-friendly, spin-friendly, or balanced.
- **Predicted First Innings Score** — e.g. *Predicted 1st Innings Score: 172–180*.

---

## 6. Toss Prediction (approx. 50 words)

Discuss:

- **What would each team choose?** Bat first or bowl first?
- **Support with:** recent tournament trends, venue records, dew factor, team preferences.
- **Key question: Is the toss decisive for the result?**
  - **If YES:** explain what happens if Team A wins the toss, and what happens if Team B wins the toss.
  - **If NO:** state that the toss should not significantly impact the outcome.

---

## 7. Our Winning Prediction (approx. 100 words)

Conclude the article with a strong prediction.

**Assess the match type:**
- *Based on betting odds:* Close Call Game / Clear Favourite Game / Neither of the Above.
- *Based on tournament context:* Important Game / Dead Rubber.

**Explain:** why your chosen team will win, key advantages, match implications.

**Finish with:**

> **Winning Prediction: Team A to Win**
