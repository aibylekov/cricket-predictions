England begin their three-Test series against Pakistan at Headingley in Leeds on 19 August, with Joe Root taking charge as full-time captain for the first time since 2022. This is England’s first Test assignment in the post-Ben Stokes era, and the hosts will be looking to start strongly on home soil.

England is very much out of the reckoning for the ICC World Test Championship (WTC) 2025-2027 Final spot. The Three Lions are currently placed 7th with only 4 wins and 8 losses in 13 matches. Recently in 2026, England suffered a 4-1 Ashes series loss to Australia followed by a 2-1 loss to New Zealand at home.

Despite England's loss, Ben Duckett was in great touch scoring 246 runs in 6 innings. He has been one of the crucial players in the Bazzball era.

Expect the likes of Harry Brook and Joe Root to provide vital experience in the middle-order. Despite being 35, Root is still as determined to close the gap to the legendary Sachin Tendulkar’s 15,921 runs. The England captain is second with 14,114 runs.

Meanwhile Dan Lawrence has been recalled after strong County Championship form, and Jamie Smith is expected to hold the wicketkeeper’s responsibility.

The bowling attack is led by Jofra Archer, Gus Atkinson, and Josh Tongue. The trio complements each other really well. Archer and Atkinson in tandem were bringing troubles for New Zealand with the duo bagging 11 and 10 wickets respectively.

Tongue too has impressed in the 12 Tests he has played, taking 57 wickets. Shoaib Bashir will offer spin options and Brydon Carse will add all-round balance after the retirement of the former captain Ben Stokes. Sam Cook has also earned a recall.

England Predicted Playing XI:

Ben Duckett, Emilio Gay, Joe Root (C), Harry Brook, Ollie Pope, Jamie Smith (WK), Jordan Cox, Brydon Carse, Gus Atkinson, Jofra Archer, Shoaib Bashir.

Pakistan Preview

Pakistan arrive in England for a three-Test series with Babar Azam back as captain. This is their first Test series in England since 2020 and forms an important part of their World Test Championship campaign where they sit even below the hosts England. In three series, they have played 6 Tests in which they have won 2 matches and lost 4.

In 2026 so far, they have played 4 Tests in which they at first suffered a shocking 2-0 defeat to hosts Bangladesh. That was followed by a 1-1 series draw against West Indies at the Caribbean islands.

Babar Azam’s return as the captain of the team is a big step taken by the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB). The Pakistani star batter has played 64 Tests in which he has scored 4674 runs. Despite the side's struggles in 2026, Babar scored 115 runs in 2 innings vs Bangladesh, followed by 193 runs in 4 innings vs West Indies.

Babar leads a side that includes experienced opener Imam-ul-Haq, Shan Masood, with Mohammad Rizwan as the wicketkeeper-batter. Both Imam and Rizwan delivered pretty horrid performances in the last two series.

For Shan Masood, the saving grace is that he scored 109 runs in the last inning vs West Indies. There will be high hopes from Abdullah Shafique, who was Pakistan next batter after Babar with 184 runs in 2 innings including an innings of 160 unbeaten facing 323 balls.

Salman Ali Agha and Aamir Jamal provide all-round options, while Saud Shakeel is in the squad subject to fitness.

Pakistan is renowned for their world-class pace bowling, but none the likes of Shaheen Afridi, Naseem Shah are featuring for the team. PCB selectors stated that they decided to drop Shaheen for his “lack of pace”. Meanwhile Naseem has failed to establish his place in the team while Hasan Ali has also been left out.

In their absence, 36-year-old Mohammad Abbas is going to lead the pace bowling attack. With 118 wickets in the bag, all eyes will be on the right-arm pacer to maximize in the English conditions. He will find support in Khurram Shahzad and Mohammad Ali.

Off-spin bowler Sajid Khan has been a big hit for Pakistan since he made his debut in 2021. In 16 matches, Sajid has 77 wickets. Salman Ali Agha and Ali Usman can also contribute a few overs of spin bowling when needed.

Pakistan Predicted Playing XI:

Imam-ul-Haq, Azan Awais, Abdullah Shafique, Babar Azam (C), Shan Masood, Mohammad Rizwan (WK), Aamir Jamal, Sajid Khan, Mohammad Abbas, Khurram Shahzad, Mohammad Ali.

PITCH AND WEATHER CONDITIONS

Weather Conditions

Expect mostly cloudy conditions with a chance of showers or light rain on several days. Daytime highs will be around 20°C, with overnight lows near 10°C. Winds will be light to moderate at approx 8-10kmph, mainly from the west. There is some possibility of rain on some days, so interruptions are possible.

Toss: To Bowl

Toss might not be a significant factor at the venue. But the captain who wins the toss, might opt to bowl first. There have been 3 Tests played at the venue since 2022 and all the games have been won by the bowling first side, so there's that mental advantage.

ENG vs PAK Winning Prediction

Both the teams have a horrid form in the WTC 2025-27 cycle. England though has a slight advantage. They have a great core set of players. Ben Duckett and Harry Brook’s (averages 93.44 vs Pakistan) performance with the bat combined with Joe Root brilliance in the middle-order. They also have a great pace trio of Archer (11 wickets in the last series), Atkinson and Tongue.

Pakistan have players who have struggled to find consistent performance whereas their main pacers have been dropped from the squad. So back England to win and take a 1-0 lead at home.
